VINCREATES — SIMPLE UPDATE GUIDE

YOUR FULL-HD / 4K MEDIA
You can use your own compressed Full-HD or 4K MP4 files directly.

REPLACE A FEATURED VIDEO
1. Copy your MP4 into: assets/videos/
2. Open projects.js in Notepad or VS Code.
3. Find the project title.
4. Change only its video path, for example:
   video:'assets/videos/my-new-jbl-fullhd.mp4'
5. Save and refresh the website.

REPLACE A THUMBNAIL
1. Copy the JPG/PNG into assets/.
2. In index.html, find the project title and change the img src path.
3. In projects.js, change the matching poster path.

ADD CASE-STUDY IMAGES
1. Copy images into assets/gallery/.
2. In projects.js add them to gallery:[...] for that project.

3D & CGI SHOWREEL
When the reel is finished, copy it to:
   assets/showreel/vincreates-3d-showreel.mp4
The site currently keeps a reserved showreel preview. The final player will be wired in before publishing.

IMPORTANT
- Use MP4/H.264 for the widest browser compatibility.
- Keep filenames simple: lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens.
- Do not delete a media file until its reference has been changed in projects.js/index.html.
- Keep master-quality originals backed up separately.
